# SKYGOLD — 24-Point Investment Review

**Company:** Sky Gold and Diamonds Limited  
**Symbol:** SKYGOLD  
**Review date:** 2026-10-01  
**Checklist:** Master 24-point investment checklist  
**Approved thesis:** v1 — Cash-flow inflection  
**Price reference:** ₹810.65 (2026-10-01)  
**Market cap reference:** ~₹12,555 crore

> This is a dated research snapshot. It should be refreshed when new results, filings, shareholding data or material price changes occur.

## Current view

Strong operating momentum with clear evidence of revenue, earnings and margin outperformance. The original cash-flow thesis has started to improve, but sustainable operating cash conversion and material debt reduction still need multiple quarters of proof. Valuation has rerated materially since the thesis reference price, so execution now matters more.

### What is working

- Q1 FY27 revenue +77.9% YoY, EBITDA +119.6% and reported PAT +140.7%.
- Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin improved to 7.8% from 6.3%; PAT margin improved to 5.2% from 3.9%.
- Operating cash flow turned positive at approximately ₹30 crore in Q1 FY27 after negative FY26 cash flow.
- Advance-gold mix increased to 17% of volume in Jun-26 from 11.5% in Mar-26 and 5.7% in Mar-25.
- Latest disclosed institutional ownership trend was positive, with FII/FPI and mutual-fund ownership increasing in Jun-26.

### What still needs proof

- One positive cash-flow quarter is not enough to prove the cash-conversion thesis.
- Net debt was approximately ₹540 crore at Q1 FY27 end; management still needs to demonstrate the targeted deleveraging.
- Governance deserves continued monitoring after the auditor transition, related-party acquisition history, exchange compliance fine, and the ₹10.7 crore incident discussed on the Q1 call.
- At roughly ₹12,555 crore market cap, FY27 management guidance implies approximately 32.6x-34.4x forward P/E, materially above the valuation at the original thesis reference price.

## Legend

- ✅ **PASS** — current evidence supports the check
- ⚠️ **WATCH** — acceptable/promising, but a meaningful caveat or proof point remains
- ❌ **FAIL** — current evidence contradicts the check or raises a material concern
- ❓ **UNKNOWN** — evidence is insufficient
- 📍 **INFORMATIONAL** — record the value rather than score it

## 1. Industry and market position

### 1.1 ⚠️ sector growth

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** The organised jewellery sector is growing in revenue, but current growth is heavily price-led rather than volume-led.

**Evidence:** CRISIL expects organised gold-jewellery retail revenue to grow 20-25% in FY27 while sales volume falls 13-15% because of high gold prices and import-policy changes. The structural shift from unorganised to organised players remains supportive for Sky Gold's customer base.

### 1.2 ✅ share gains

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** Sky Gold appears to be gaining share / wallet share substantially faster than its sector and listed peer set.

**Evidence:** Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue grew 77.9% YoY. Value Research reports TTM revenue growth of 81.4% versus a peer median of 22.17%. This is not a precise audited market-share calculation, but the growth differential is strong evidence of share gains.

### 1.3 ✅ better than industry

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** The differentiated proposition is design-led lightweight jewellery, broad product capability, fast manufacturing cycles, large organised-retailer relationships and the capital-light advance-gold model.

**Evidence:** Company disclosures cite ~180 design professionals, 10-20% lower-weight products, value-added mix of ~50-55% in FY26, ~1,200 kg monthly capacity, and product capability covering ~80% of a typical retail store's mix. These competitive-advantage claims are mostly company-reported and should keep being tested against peers.

## 2. Business quality

### 2.1 ✅ core proven

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** The core B2B jewellery-manufacturing business is proven and scaled.

**Evidence:** FY26 consolidated revenue was ₹6,294.9 crore and PAT ₹281.8 crore, with customers including large organised jewellery retailers in India and overseas.

### 2.2 ⚠️ advantage

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** There are credible operating advantages, but it is too early to call them an unassailable moat.

**Evidence:** Design capability, retailer relationships, manufacturing speed, product breadth and lightweight/value-added capabilities are tangible. However, the B2B manufacturing market remains fragmented and many differentiation claims come from management disclosures.

## 3. Growth

### 3.1 ✅ growth revenue

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** Revenue is growing very strongly.

**Evidence:** FY26 revenue grew 77.4% YoY to ₹6,294.9 crore. Q1 FY27 revenue grew another 77.9% YoY to ₹2,012.8 crore.

### 3.2 ✅ growth profit

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** Profit is growing faster than revenue.

**Evidence:** FY26 PAT grew 112.4% YoY to ₹281.8 crore. Q1 FY27 reported PAT grew 140.7% YoY to ₹104.9 crore.

### 3.3 ✅ growth visibility

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** Near-term growth visibility is strong, although it relies partly on management guidance.

**Evidence:** Management guides FY27 revenue of approximately ₹8,100 crore with EBITDA margin of 7.0-7.5% and PAT margin of 4.5-4.75%. Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹2,012.8 crore is already close to that annualised run-rate. Export orders of ₹30-45 crore from UK/Europe buyers were also disclosed.

## 4. Margins and cash generation

### 4.1 ✅ margins

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** Margins are improving.

**Evidence:** Q1 FY27 gross margin rose to 9.3% from 8.1% YoY, EBITDA margin to 7.8% from 6.3%, and PAT margin to 5.2% from 3.9%. FY26 EBITDA margin was approximately 6.9% versus 5.5% in FY25.

### 4.2 ⚠️ cash conversion

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** Cash conversion is improving sharply, but the thesis is not proven yet.

**Evidence:** FY26 consolidated operating cash flow was approximately negative ₹45 crore versus negative ₹272 crore in FY25. Q1 FY27 operating cash flow turned positive at approximately ₹30 crore. The next test is whether positive cash flow persists while growth remains high.

## 5. Balance sheet and returns

### 5.1 ⚠️ debt

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** Debt looks manageable, but meaningful deleveraging is still a forward promise rather than a completed result.

**Evidence:** Q1 FY27 net debt was approximately ₹540 crore. Management previously targeted FY27 net debt of roughly ₹200-250 crore and a 50-55% reduction through operating improvement and asset monetisation. This should be checked quarter by quarter.

### 5.2 ✅ returns

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** Returns on capital/equity are attractive and improving.

**Evidence:** Screener reports ROCE of approximately 23.4% and ROE of 23.9%. The FY26 annual report's standalone ratios show ROCE 19.69% versus 15.80% in FY25 and ROE 23.88%.

## 6. Management and governance

### 6.1 ✅ walked talk

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** Management has materially exceeded several earlier operating targets.

**Evidence:** In FY23 management guided to roughly ₹5,000 crore FY27 revenue and ~3.5% PAT margin. FY26 already delivered ₹6,295 crore revenue and ~4.5% PAT margin, and FY27 guidance was raised to ₹8,100 crore and 4.5-4.75% PAT margin. Cash-flow improvement has also arrived earlier than the original FY27-28 neutrality plan, though durability remains unproven.

### 6.2 ⚠️ governance

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** Governance has improved in some areas, but there are enough recent events to justify continued scrutiny rather than a clean pass.

**Evidence:** The prior statutory auditor resigned in May 2026, with disclosed reasons tied to expanded scope/professional commitments and no stated disagreement; MSKA (a BDO member firm) was appointed. Earlier acquisitions of Starmangalsutra and Sparkling Chains were related-party transactions. In July 2026 the company disclosed an exchange-compliance fine and strengthened controls. The Q1 call also discussed a ₹10.7 crore incident, with ~₹3.5 crore recovered and legal/investigative proceedings continuing.

### 6.3 ✅ ownership flow

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** The latest ownership trend is supportive.

**Evidence:** Promoter holding was stable at 51.74% in Jun-26 with zero pledge. FII/FPI ownership rose from 1.20% to 2.79% QoQ, mutual funds from 10.33% to 11.03%, and total institutional ownership from 14.34% to 16.99%. Historical promoter-percentage dilution before Sep-25 should still be understood separately from open-market promoter selling.

## 7. Valuation

### 7.1 ⚠️ valuation vs growth

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** Growth is strong enough to support a premium valuation, but the stock is no longer obviously cheap.

**Evidence:** At approximately ₹12,555 crore market cap, FY27 guidance of ₹8,100 crore revenue and 4.5-4.75% PAT margin implies PAT of roughly ₹365-385 crore and a forward P/E of about 32.6x-34.4x. This requires continued high growth, margin delivery and improving cash conversion.

### 7.2 ⚠️ embedded expectations

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** A meaningful part of the improvement is now reflected in the share price.

**Evidence:** The approved thesis used a reference price of ₹500 on 24-Jun-26. The stock was around ₹810.65 on 1-Oct-26, roughly 62% higher. The market is therefore paying substantially more for the same cash-flow/deleveraging thesis and expects execution.

### 7.3 ⚠️ downside

**Assessment:** WATCH

**Answer:** Business solvency does not currently look like the key downside; valuation compression and a failure of cash conversion are the larger stock risks.

**Evidence:** Profitability and return ratios are strong, but net debt remains meaningful and the forward multiple is above 30x management-guided earnings. A miss on growth, margins or cash flow could therefore cause a substantial de-rating even without a balance-sheet crisis.

### 8.1 ✅ price trend

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** The long- and medium-term trend is technically strong.

**Evidence:** Around 1-Oct-26 the stock was ₹810.65 versus approximately ₹767.70 50-DMA and ₹504.33 200-DMA; the 50-DMA was well above the 200-DMA.

### 8.2 📍 support resistance

**Assessment:** INFORMATIONAL

**Evidence:** The support zone is anchored around the recent 50-DMA / technical pivots, while ₹868.8 is the reported 52-week and all-time high. These levels move with price and should be refreshed before any trade.

### 8.3 ✅ index outperformance

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** The stock has massively outperformed the broad small-cap benchmark.

**Evidence:** As of 18-Aug-26, Sky Gold had returned about 112% over six months and 182% over 12 months. Around the same period, Nifty Smallcap 250 index funds showed roughly 14% six-month and 9-10% one-year returns.

## 9. Thesis and catalyst

### 9.1 ✅ main driver

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** The main value driver is clear: convert rapid B2B growth into self-funded growth through advance gold, higher-value product mix, positive operating cash flow and lower debt.

**Evidence:** This matches the approved Thesis v1 and current management strategy.

### 9.2 ✅ falsifiers

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** The thesis has explicit falsifiers.

**Evidence:** Key breaks include sustained negative operating cash flow, deterioration in working-capital economics, failure to reduce debt/interest burden, or customer/governance events that damage economics or minority-shareholder interests.

### 9.3 ✅ catalyst

**Assessment:** PASS

**Answer:** There is a clear evidence path for the next re-rating or de-rating.

**Evidence:** Key catalysts are sustained positive CFO, visible net-debt reduction toward FY27 targets, higher advance-gold mix, export/diamond growth, continued margin expansion and delivery against revised FY27 guidance.

## Bottom line

SKYGOLD currently passes most operating-growth and business-quality checks. The investment case has moved from **“can it grow?”** toward **“can this growth consistently convert into cash and lower debt?”** Q1 FY27 is the first strong positive data point on that question, not yet final proof.

At the same time, the stock has rerated sharply from the thesis reference price. That makes **cash conversion, deleveraging and governance execution** more important now than simply reporting another high revenue-growth quarter.
